## LIFT Experimental Cinematic Intro

The opening shoe-preparation and running footage (`opening-card.mp4`, with `opening-poster.jpg`) is by [MART PRODUCTION — Man is Tying his Rubber Shoes, Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/video/man-is-tying-his-rubber-shoes-7884017/). The clip serves as one continuous human anchor for the prepare and move beats.

Fuel and recovery are introduced as brief, restrained photographic moments using `nutrition.jpg` and `foam-recovery.jpg`, both also present in the existing Hero image language. A fine progress mark and short adaptation cue communicate how LIFT responds to a person's time and recovery needs. The source footage, licensed stock images and muted green grade are used to maintain a cohesive film treatment. Podium informed pacing and cinematic restraint only; no Podium media, branding or layout is used.

Earlier opening experiments remain on disk but are not referenced or loaded by the new intro.
