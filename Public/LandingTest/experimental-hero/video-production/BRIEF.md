## Ready-to-use generation prompt

Create a photorealistic cinematic opening for LIFT, a premium fitness and nutrition platform. Use the supplied runner references consistently: the same young adult male athlete, same face and hairstyle, black long-sleeve athletic top, black shorts and white running shoes, in the same leafy urban park atmosphere.

ONE CONTINUOUS CAMERA TAKE. Begin at a low three-quarter angle as the athlete finishes tying his running shoe. The knot is already almost complete; show one natural final pull. The athlete stands naturally and turns his face toward the lens. The camera rises with him and smoothly dollies toward his face, maintaining natural perspective and consistent light. He pauses and looks calmly into the camera. The camera continues toward ONE visible eye, moving from a facial close-up to an extreme macro view of that SAME eye. Keep the eye centered as the camera passes through the pupil into near-black with a subtle deep forest-green undertone. End in this dark frame so a separate existing web animation can be revealed in editing.

Aim for roughly 10–12 seconds if supported: 0–3 seconds shoe preparation; 3–6 seconds rising and approaching the face; 6–9 seconds continuous close-up into the eye; final 1–2 seconds pupil entry and dark endpoint. Prioritize coherent anatomy and uninterrupted camera motion over exact timings. Cinematic controlled lighting, natural skin texture, gentle depth of field, restrained forest-green environmental tones, premium realistic advertising photography. Center the face and eye in a crop-safe region for a square or portrait hero panel.

Do not introduce another person, change the actor's face or clothes, cut to unrelated eye footage, dissolve between different faces, morph the face, freeze the actor, perform a digital pixel zoom, hide a cut with heavy blur, warp the eyelids, duplicate pupils, add a sci-fi tunnel, text, logos, interface elements or the image sphere. The image sphere already exists on the website and will be revealed separately. Silent output is sufficient.
