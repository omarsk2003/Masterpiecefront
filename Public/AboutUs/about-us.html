01What is LIFT?

LIFT stands for Lifestyle & Intelligent Fitness Technology. It is a personalized fitness and nutrition platform designed to build guidance around your goals, lifestyle, schedule, health, habits, and available time.

02How does LIFT build my plan?

LIFT starts by learning about you — your goals, fitness level, schedule, available workout time, health considerations, food habits, and whether you train at home or in a gym. Those details are used to shape a more personalized workout and nutrition experience.

03What happens if my schedule changes?

LIFT is designed around real life. When your availability or routine changes, the plan can adapt so your next step still feels realistic and manageable.

04Does LIFT include both workout and nutrition guidance?

Yes. LIFT brings movement and nutrition together in one experience so both can support the same goals and lifestyle.

05What is the difference between Free, AI Plan, and Premium?

LIFT uses one client account with different levels of support. Free provides access to the core LIFT experience. AI Plan adds personalized AI-powered workout and nutrition planning. Premium adds a higher level of support, including professional guidance where available.

06What does Premium add?

Premium is designed for clients who want more human support alongside LIFT's technology. A coach can focus on workout guidance, while a nutritionist can support the nutrition side of the plan.

07Can a coach or nutritionist change my plan?

Within the Premium experience, a coach can review and support the training side of the plan, while a nutritionist can review and support nutrition. Each professional stays focused on their own area of expertise.

08Is LIFT suitable for beginners?

Yes. LIFT starts from your current level, available time, goals, and personal context, so the experience can be shaped around where you are starting rather than assuming everyone begins at the same level.
