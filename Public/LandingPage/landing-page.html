PLANS

See how the plans compare.

Free covers tracking and general guidance. AI Plan adds personalized workout and nutrition plans shaped by your goals, schedule and progress.

Premium includes everything in AI Plan, plus a coach and nutritionist who review and support your plan.

01 / FREE PLAN

Build your foundation.

Free Plan gives you a simple place to build consistency. You can record workouts and meals, follow your activity, and see how your progress changes over time. The workout and nutrition areas offer general guidance that helps you start and maintain a routine.

This plan works without a detailed lifestyle profile. Its guidance stays broad, so it does not create a workout or nutrition plan around your personal schedule, goals, or food preferences. A coach and nutritionist are not part of this level.

02 / AI PLAN

Make the plan yours.

AI Plan includes the activity and progress tools in Free Plan, then adds personalization. When you first use the AI Assistant, you provide your goals, fitness level, available days and time, training location, health considerations, and food habits. LIFT uses that context to shape workout and nutrition plan previews for your routine.

As your schedule or progress changes, the guidance can reflect the details you have saved and the activity you log. You can update your context in the AI Assistant whenever your routine changes. This level gives you personalized AI guidance without professional review or specialist conversations.

03 / PREMIUM

Add people to your plan.

Premium includes the personalized AI experience and adds professional support. A coach focuses on your training plan, while a nutritionist focuses on your food plan. They can review the relevant guidance and help you adjust it when your needs change.

You can message each specialist separately about your training or nutrition. Your coach can also propose a meeting time, which you can accept or decline in the Meetings page. Premium is for people who want human guidance alongside the AI plan.
