Where do I talk to my coach or nutritionist?+

Open Messages from your navigation. Sarah handles your training plan, and Maya handles your nutrition plan. Each conversation is separate.

How do I request a change to my plan?+

Discuss the change with the relevant specialist in Messages. They can review it with you before an updated plan is approved.

Open Coach Zoom meetings from your dashboard. If a meeting link does not work, choose Video & meetings above to prepare a support request.
